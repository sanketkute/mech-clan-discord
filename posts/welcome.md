**Clan**

**Get started**
1. Read {rules}.
2. Link your game account: type `/link create` in {chat} and enter your player tag (it's under your name in your game profile). You get your Town Hall and clan rank roles automatically, and war reminders can ping you when you have attacks left.
3. Say hi in {chat}.

**Where things are**
{announcements}: news from leadership
{cwl}: CWL sign-ups and lineups
{capital-raids}: raid weekend
{bases}: base links by Town Hall

Discord is optional for clan members. Everything important is also shared in game.
Questions? Ask a Leader or Co-Leader.
