# Clan rules

### Clan Wars
1. Opt in only when your heroes are free, and use both attacks.
2. Can't attack after opting in? Tell clan chat early.

### CWL
1. Sign up before each season in clan chat or {cwl}.
2. Only TH11+ accounts make the active roster.
3. Missed attacks mean removal from the roster.
4. Bonus medals go to the top star earners.

### Raid Weekend
1. Use all 6 capital attacks every raid weekend.
2. Finish the district you start, unless you're out of attacks.

### Activity
1. Inactive 14 days without notice: you may be removed.
2. Elder: 2 active weeks with no missed war attacks.

### Server
1. Be respectful. No insults, harassment or drama.
2. No spam, ads or suspicious links. "Free Nitro" and "Steam gift" links are scams: report them.
3. Keep it family-friendly: no NSFW content.
4. Leadership decisions are final. Questions? Ask a Leader or Co-Leader.
