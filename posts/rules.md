# Rules

### Server
1. Be respectful. No insults, harassment or drama.
2. No spam, advertising or suspicious links. "Free Nitro" or "Steam gift" links are scams; report them to a Co-Leader.
3. Keep it family-friendly: no NSFW content.
4. Use the right channel for the topic.
5. Leadership decisions are final. Questions or problems: message a Co-Leader.

### Clan Wars
1. Set your war preference to "In" only if you can attack with your heroes available, and use both attacks.
2. Can't attack after opting in? Say so in clan chat as early as you can.

### CWL
1. Tell leadership you want in before each season, in clan chat or {cwl}.
2. Only TH11+ accounts make the active roster.
3. Missed attacks mean removal from the roster.
4. Bonus medals go to the top star earners.

### Raid Weekend
1. Use all 6 capital attacks every raid weekend.

### Activity
1. Inactive for 14 days without telling leadership means you may be removed.
2. Elder is earned after 2 weeks of activity with no missed war attacks.
